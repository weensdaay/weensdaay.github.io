About the numbers & sources+

Public pages only. Coverage is shown under each total; unavailable counters are never treated as zero. One person may follow more than one account. The ≈ sign means a platform rounds its count. Total views sum the available YouTube counters.

Growth over 7 and 365 days is the difference between two public counter snapshots, not a private analytics report. Until the baseline exists, we build history and show a dash in account cards. Growth is withheld when a counter decreases due to removals or corrections.

If a source is unavailable, its last successful dated snapshot remains. Values older than two days are excluded from totals. Music streams and likes are not mixed with video views.
